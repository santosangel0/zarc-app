# EMBRAPA — Empresa Brasileira de Pesquisa Agropecuária

Brazilian Agricultural Research Corporation. Federal research agency under [[mapa|MAPA]], with decentralized research centers across Brazil.

## Key Units for This Research

- **EMBRAPA Gado de Leite** (Juiz de Fora, MG) — dairy cattle research. Lead institution behind [[andrade-2023-thi-maps-southeast-brazil|Andrade et al. (2023)]]. Hosts the GeoInfo platform.
- **EMBRAPA Cerrados** (Planaltina, DF) — published thermal comfort guidelines for dairy cattle on pasture (Ferreira et al. 2017, Documentos 342).

## GeoInfo Platform

EMBRAPA maintains **GeoInfo**, a geospatial metadata and map portal. The monthly [[thi-temperature-humidity-index|THI]] maps from Andrade et al. (2023) are published there:
- http://geoinfo.cnpgl.embrapa.br/maps/998

- **EMBRAPA Monitoramento por Satélite** (Campinas, SP; now EMBRAPA Territorial) — remote sensing and spatial monitoring. [[leivas-2014-svi-drought-soybean-south-brazil|Leivas et al. (2014)]] used SPOT-Vegetation NDVI for drought monitoring in Southern Brazil.

## CILeite Platform

[[cileite|CILeite]] (Centro de Inteligência do Leite) is an EMBRAPA Gado de Leite platform providing municipality-level dairy production data (production volume, cow density, productivity per animal). Used by [[dias-souza-2026-dairy-climate-minas-gerais|Dias-Souza et al. (2026)]] to identify high-productivity municipalities in Minas Gerais.

## Research Continuity

Ricardo Guimarães Andrade (EMBRAPA Gado de Leite) appears across multiple studies: THI mapping ([[andrade-2023-thi-maps-southeast-brazil|2023]]), drought indices ([[silva-2025-drought-indices-paracatu|2025]]), and SVI drought monitoring ([[leivas-2014-svi-drought-soybean-south-brazil|2014]]) — indicating a sustained research line connecting remote sensing, climate indices, and agricultural monitoring.

## Role in ZARC

EMBRAPA provides the **technical research** underlying the ZARC (Zoneamento Agrícola de Risco Climático) system. MAPA publishes the official portarias; EMBRAPA develops the models, data, and methodology.
