## Entities

- [[embrapa]] — Brazilian Agricultural Research Corporation; key units: Gado de Leite, Cerrados, Monitoramento por Satélite. GeoInfo and CILeite platforms.
- [[inmet]] — National meteorological institute; automatic weather station network providing temperature/humidity data.
- [[ibge-ppm]] — Municipal Livestock Survey; official milk production statistics via SIDRA API.
- [[cileite]] — Centro de Inteligência do Leite; EMBRAPA platform for municipality-level dairy production data.

## Concepts

- [[thi-temperature-humidity-index]] — Temperature and Humidity Index; primary metric for dairy thermal comfort. Thresholds: ≤70 comfort, ≥72 production risk. NOAA formula variant (threshold 76 for crossbred tropicals).
- [[thermal-stress-dairy]] — Heat stress mechanisms, breed-dependent thermoneutral zones, genetic antagonism with production, precipitation interaction.
- [[idw-interpolation]] — Inverse distance weighting spatial interpolation; used for THI surface generation.
- [[ndvi-vegetation-indices]] — NDVI and SVI; remote sensing vegetation indices for drought and pasture monitoring.
- [[spi-spei-drought-indices]] — Standardized Precipitation Index and SPEI; drought quantification at multiple time scales.
- [[precipitation-dairy-production]] — Precipitation as a climate driver of dairy production via forage availability; evidence from MG.
